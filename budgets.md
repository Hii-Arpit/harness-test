# Budgets

Budgets set a spending limit and show when cost exceeds it, or when cost is forecasted to exceed it.

**Budgets** is one page with two tabs:

* **Resource budgets** tracks cloud spend against a cost view.
* **User budgets for AI spend** sets a per-user limit on AI providers.

Click **New budget**, then select **Resource budget** or **User budget for AI spend**.

In the left navigation, select **Cloud & AI Costs**, then select **Budgets**.
